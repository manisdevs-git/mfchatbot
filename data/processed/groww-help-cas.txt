What is CAS and why do I need it?
CAS stands for Consolidated Account Statement 📊, issued by CAMS/KFin Technologies. It's a detailed statement that summarizes all your investments linked to your email ID and PAN, covering various financial products like Mutual Funds, Stocks, and other securities.
Why is CAS important?
Track Investments:
It helps you see all your investments in one place.
Transparency:
It gives a clear view of your portfolio, making it easy to monitor performance.
Tax Filing:
It's handy for tax filing as it shows your earnings and capital gains.
Keep your CAS handy to stay on top of your investment game!
