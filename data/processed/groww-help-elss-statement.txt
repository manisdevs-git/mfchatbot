What is a mutual funds ELSS report?
Loading...
The ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) report shows your purchases in ELSS mutual funds and the total amount you invested in the selected financial year.
Here's how you can download it:
Go to your profile.
Select the 'Reports' option.
Under 'Tax', find 'Mutual Fund - ELSS statement'.
Click on 'Download'.
Note: The password to open the report is your PAN number in capital letters.
