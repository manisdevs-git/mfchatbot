## 8. Process (statements)

**Q:** How do I download an ELSS tax statement?

**A:** On Groww, open your profile, choose Reports, then under Tax find Mutual Fund - ELSS statement and click Download. The password to open the report is your PAN in capital letters.

Source: https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/order/how-to-download-tax-statement--for-elss--77
